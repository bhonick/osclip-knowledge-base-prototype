# Contributing

This contribution process is provisional and will be revised by the OSCLIP Project 1 working group.

## Current phase

The internal resource-submission period has ended. The current focus is reviewing, cleaning, categorizing, and publishing the resources already collected.

Please do not add new resources directly to the repository during this phase unless the working group has agreed to include them.

## Ways to contribute during the prototype phase

Working group members can help by:

- Reviewing resource descriptions
- Checking links
- Suggesting categories or tags
- Identifying duplicate or overlapping resources
- Flagging cost, accessibility, maintenance, licensing, or sustainability concerns
- Reviewing the readability and accessibility of the proposed format

Corrections and suggestions may initially be submitted through GitHub Issues. A pull-request process may be adopted after the group has assessed members' GitHub experience and preferences.

## Future submissions

OSCLIP plans to reopen its resource-submission form when the public call for resources is published.

## Sensitive information

Do not add submitter email addresses, private working-group correspondence, or other internal information to this public repository.
