## Discovery, Publishing, and Open Access

| Resource | Description | Intended audience | Resource type(s) | Access and notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [arXiv and related preprint servers](https://arxiv.org/) | Open-access preprint repository for research in physics, mathematics, computer science, quantitative biology, statistics, and related fields; discipline-specific servers such as bioRxiv and medRxiv use similar models. | Researchers; librarians; scholarly communication staff | Tools and Platforms | Free to use; submissions are moderated but generally have not completed peer review; licensing varies by deposit. |
| [DOAJ: Directory of Open Access Journals](https://doaj.org/) | Global index of peer-reviewed open-access journals that meet DOAJ inclusion criteria. | Researchers; authors; librarians; scholarly communication staff | Tools and Platforms | Free to use; journal records include information such as article-processing charges and publishing policies. |
| [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) | Free and open index of scholarly works, authors, institutions, sources, topics, and related research entities. | Researchers; research groups; institutions; librarians; research-impact staff | Tools and Platforms | Free to use; coverage and metrics depend on the records indexed and linked by OpenAlex. |
| [OpenDOAR](https://opendoar.ac.uk/) | Directory of open-access repositories from around the world. | Researchers; librarians; repository staff; users searching for gray literature and openly available scholarship | Tools and Platforms | Free to use; repository coverage and record currency depend on directory maintenance. |
| [PREreview](https://prereview.org/) | Platform and community supporting open peer review of preprints and datasets, with training, mentorship, and citable reviews. | Researchers, especially early-career researchers; librarians; open peer-review communities | Organizations/Communities, Tools and Platforms | Free to use; an ORCID is required to submit a review; published reviews are archived on Zenodo under a CC BY license. |
| [Science Explorer (SciX)](https://scixplorer.org/) | Literature and data discovery platform connecting publications with datasets, software, and related products in Earth and space sciences. | Researchers in Earth science, planetary science, astrophysics, heliophysics, and related NASA-supported fields | Tools and Platforms | Free to search; not every linked publication or dataset is openly accessible. |
| [Unpaywall](https://unpaywall.org/) | Database, API, and browser-based service that identifies legal open-access copies of scholarly articles. | Researchers; librarians; interlibrary-loan staff; discovery-system developers; open-access compliance staff | Tools and Platforms, Educational Resources/Open Curricula | Free to use; results are limited to content indexed by Unpaywall and its underlying infrastructure. |

## Metadata, Identifiers, Standards, and Repository Discovery

| Resource | Description | Intended audience | Resource type(s) | Access and notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [CEDAR Workbench](https://metadatacenter.org/) | Platform for creating, sharing, and using structured metadata templates with controlled terms and JSON/JSON-LD outputs. | Data curators; metadata specialists; biomedical researchers; repository staff; FAIR data practitioners | Tools and Platforms | Free to use; requires familiarity with structured metadata, templates, controlled vocabularies, or JSON-LD. |
| [Contributor Role Taxonomy (CRediT)](https://credit.niso.org/) | Community-developed taxonomy of 14 contributor roles for describing how individuals contributed to research outputs. | Authors; editors; publishers; research administrators; librarians | Practical Guides, Policies/Institutional Examples | Free to access; reuse and permission terms should be reviewed before incorporating the taxonomy directly. |
| [FAIRsharing](https://fairsharing.org/) | Registry of standards, repositories, and policies. | Researchers; librarians; data stewards | Tools and Platforms | Free to use; community-maintained records. |
| [HuBMAP Metadata Documentation](https://docs.hubmapconsortium.org/metadata.html) | Specifications for metadata required across samples and assays in the Human Biomolecular Atlas Program. | Researchers; data curators; metadata specialists | Metadata standards | Free to use; uses domain-specific schemas and Excel/TSV templates and may require biomedical context. |
| [Metadata Standards Catalog](https://rdamsc.bath.ac.uk/) | Catalog of metadata standards, tools, mappings, and related resources for research data management and curation. | Librarians; data curators; repository managers; metadata specialists; researchers | Tools and Platforms | Free to use; licensing, accessibility, and long-term maintenance information need review. |
| [ORCID](https://orcid.org/) | Persistent identifier and profile system that helps distinguish researchers and connect them with publications, datasets, grants, affiliations, and other scholarly outputs. | Researchers; scholars; research administrators; librarians | Tools and Platforms | Free for individual researchers; institutional integrations and memberships may involve costs. |
| [re3data.org](https://www.re3data.org/) | Global registry of research data repositories across disciplines, with filters for repository characteristics, certifications, and metadata standards. | Researchers depositing or discovering data; librarians; data stewards | Tools and Platforms | Free to use; repository records and classifications depend on ongoing maintenance. |
| [Research Organization Registry (ROR)](https://ror.org/) | Community-governed registry assigning persistent identifiers to research organizations and resolving institutional name variations across systems. | Metadata specialists; repository and CRIS managers; librarians; funders; publishers; developers | Advocacy Resources, Tools and Platforms, Educational Resources/Open Curricula, Services | Free to use; sustainability depends on consortium support and continued community curation. |
| [RO-Crate](https://www.researchobject.org/ro-crate/) | Community-developed approach for packaging research data with structured, machine-actionable metadata using JSON-LD and Schema.org. | Data curators; repository developers; computational researchers; research software and workflow communities | Educational Resources/Open Curricula | Free and openly developed; requires technical familiarity with structured metadata, JSON, or research workflow packaging. [Source repository](https://github.com/ResearchObject/ro-crate). |

## Research Workflow and Collaboration Tools

| Resource | Description | Intended audience | Resource type(s) | Access and notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [GitHub](https://github.com/) | Cloud-based platform for version control, code and documentation hosting, collaboration, and sharing research software and related materials. | Researchers; developers; research software and data teams; librarians | Tools and Platforms | Free and paid tiers; proprietary platform owned by Microsoft; public and private repository features vary by plan. |
| [LabArchives](https://www.labarchives.com/) | Cloud-based electronic lab notebook for collecting, organizing, sharing, and publishing laboratory data and supporting collaboration and reproducibility. | Researchers; instructors; laboratory teams | Tools and Platforms | Freemium service; core free features are limited; some integrations and plugins rely on proprietary tools. |
| [OpenRefine](https://openrefine.org/) | Cleans, transforms, facets, and reconciles structured data. | Researchers; data curators; metadata professionals | Tools and Platforms | Free and open source; usually requires local installation. [Source code](https://github.com/OpenRefine/OpenRefine). |
| [OSF Preregistration](https://help.osf.io/article/330-welcome-to-registrations) | Creates a time-stamped record of a study plan before research begins and can connect later outputs and outcomes to that record. | Researchers; reviewers; research-support professionals | Tools and Platforms | Free to use; licensing and accessibility details need review. |
| [protocols.io](https://www.protocols.io/) | Collaborative platform for creating, versioning, running, and sharing detailed research methods and protocols. | Researchers using step-by-step experimental or computational procedures; research-support staff | Tools and Platforms | Free tier is limited for private protocols; paid plans may be costly; public protocols use CC BY; owned by Springer Nature. |
